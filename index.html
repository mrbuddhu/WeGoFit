Meet Coach TinaBarks

For over a decade, I've worked a demanding professional career while maintaining a healthy lifestyle and sustainable weight. Along the way, I watched friends and colleagues struggle with preventable health challenges—not because they lacked motivation, but because they lacked practical support and accountability.

That experience inspired WeGoFit. My mission is to help busy people build healthy habits that fit into real life, not perfect life. Because your greatest wealth is your health.
